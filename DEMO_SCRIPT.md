# Scout demo video — shot list (~100 seconds)

Record on the phone (screen record, portrait). Battery decent, notifications off
(Do Not Disturb), dark theme. If a live WC match is on when you record, even better.

## Prep before recording
1. Fresh install of the final APK.
2. Download **Qwen3 0.6B (Instant)** and **SmolVLM2 500M** beforehand off-camera
   (downloads are boring on video), but do NOT load a model yet.
3. Have a jersey photo in the gallery (or wear one).
4. Make 2-3 predictions the day before so the W/L record chip has numbers.

## Shots

**1. Cold open — the hook (0:00-0:15)**
- Turn ON airplane mode on camera. Open Scout.
- Home: "Qwen3 0.6B ready · Load Model" → tap Load Model → loads in seconds.
- Open AI Coach, ask "What is a false nine?" → tokens stream instantly.
- (Spoken/caption: "A full football AI. Airplane mode. Nothing leaves the phone.")

**2. Tool calling — the technical beat (0:15-0:40)**
- Airplane mode OFF.
- Ask the Coach: "Who is playing today?"
- Show the green "Fetching live data..." chip → answer lists real fixtures.
- (Caption: "The on-device model calls tools: live fixtures, team form — then
  grounds its answer.")

**3. Deep thinking (0:40-0:55)**
- Toggle Deep. Ask "Will Brazil win the World Cup?"
- Show the amber thinking streaming live, then collapsing to "Thought for Xs".

**4. Predictor + accountability (0:55-1:20)**
- Open Predictor: show the matchday rail (live scores if any), W/L record chip
  in the header.
- Tap the WC fixture → live form dots appear → Predict Match.
- Show the streaming chips (winner → score → confidence), then the scoreboard,
  players to watch, analysis.
- (Caption: "Every prediction is graded against the real result. The AI keeps
  a public track record.")

**5. Scout Lens multimodal (1:20-1:40)**
- Scan the jersey photo → identification appears.
- Ask a follow-up: "Which era is this kit from?" → answer streams.
- (Caption: "Vision + text conversation. On-device.")

**6. Close (1:40-1:45)**
- Settings page: scroll past Engine CPU/GPU and the QVAC privacy card.
- End card: "Scout — 100% on-device football AI. QVAC SDK. 170 MB APK."

## Don'ts
- Don't show model downloads or long Gemma loads.
- Don't linger on empty screens; cut waits.
- Don't demo GPU mode.
